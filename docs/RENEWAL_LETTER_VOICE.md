# Renewal letters — voice

Distilled from the two written on 3 Aug 2026 (Dhanishta, Samaa) and the
coach's own corrections to them. The rules exist because each was got wrong
first.

## Structure

1. **Open with something true about them**, from their own data — their score,
   their words, what they worked out on the last call.
2. **Say what the plan was for**, and that it is finished.
3. **Say what takes longer**, and why — the clinical reason to continue.
4. **Then, and only then, the price.**
5. **Close with a call.**

## Rules, each from a correction

- **No "I want to be straight with you."** Reads as confrontation; wrong
  register in Indian correspondence entirely.
- **Never name the supplement being held back.** Say there is one. Naming it
  hands over the offer.
- **No "shall I send you the details?"** Ask for a call.
- **No "I'm around all week."**
- **Never quote app-usage statistics** — "you opened it 23 of 28 days" reads
  as surveillance. Say "you've stayed with it" instead: same fact, told as a
  compliment.
- **Do not use a family member's own diagnosis as leverage**, even when it is
  the most persuasive line available. Too personal.
- **Never imply the practice has been careless.** "You've been taking it
  without us ever measuring it" is true and unusable; "the point of a loading
  dose is to see where it lands before settling on maintenance" is the same
  fact, correctly framed.
- **Name what has NOT moved.** "The scale hasn't moved much, and I won't
  pretend otherwise" is what earns the rest of the letter.
- **₹, not Rs**, in anything the coach approves.

## Household

Check for family members renewing nearby BEFORE drafting. A mother received a
request for ₹50,000 for her daughter six days before her own renewal was due,
because nobody looked.
